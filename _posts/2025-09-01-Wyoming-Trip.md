During the
labor day long
weekend, my
friends and I
went to
Wyoming. On
this trip, we
visited Grand
Teton National
Park,
Yellowstone
National Park,
and Devils
Tower. Devils
Tower is the
first national
monument in
the United
States.
Although it
wasn’t part of
our original
plan, thanks
to two smart
people in our
group
(obviously not
me), we
improvised and
added it to
the itinerary.

We made some
great
memories—canoeing
at Jenny Lake,
watching Old
Faithful erupt
together, and
admiring
Artist Point
in
Yellowstone. I
also bought a
national parks
passport, with
the goal of
collecting
stamps as I
visit
different
parks. The
ultimate dream
is to get a
stamp from
every national
park in the
U.S.

Wyoming is
about a
13-hour drive
from Lincoln.
Crazy, right?
But since
almost all of
us took turns
driving, it
didn’t feel
overwhelming.
I’m looking
forward to
making more
trips like
this during my
PhD journey.
