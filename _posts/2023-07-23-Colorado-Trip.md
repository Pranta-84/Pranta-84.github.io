At the end of
July 2023, I
went on a
three-day trip
to Colorado,
USA. During
the trip, I
visited Estes
Park, the
Alpine Visitor
Center, Pikes
Peak, Seven
Falls, and
Grand Lake—so
many places in
just three
days! This was
my first trip
within the US
since coming
to the
University of
Nebraska–Lincoln
for my MS in
2022.


I was the only
person in the
group who
didn’t know
how to
drive—lol—so
the rest of
the group
handled all
the driving. I
couldn’t help
but wonder
what it would
feel like to
drive on those
beautiful
roads in
Colorado.
